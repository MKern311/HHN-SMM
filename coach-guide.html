Talk through your SOSTAC® plan with Claude

Social Media Management · Hochschule Heilbronn · works with your own Claude account (Free is enough)

What the coach does

  • Claude reads your plan through the SOSTAC Coach connector: your entries, the task of each block and the criteria of the course.
  • It coaches: it asks questions, checks your entries against the criteria and points to evidence in your Situation analysis.
  • Only when you ask and confirm the wording, it can write a text into a single field or add posts to your content calendar. Your team stays the author.
  • Everything Claude writes is listed in the tool under Situation › Our Approach, so you can label AI support.

1 · Add the connector (once)

  1. Open claude.ai and sign in with your own account.
  2. Go to Settings › Connectors › Add custom connector.
  3. Name: SOSTAC Coach · URL:
    https://earwxoxayapshwgnuxds.supabase.co/functions/v1/sostac-coach
    Leave everything else empty and save.
  4. The connector works in the browser and in the Claude apps on your phone and laptop.

The Free plan allows one custom connector. If you already use one, remove it while you work on your plan.

2 · Start a conversation

From the tool: scroll to the block you are working on and click Discuss with Claude (bottom right). Claude opens with a first message that names your group code and the block.

Directly in Claude: switch the connector on in the chat (tools icon below the input field) and write, for example:

Open our SOSTAC plan with the code group-04 and help me with Persona 1. Ask me questions, do not write it for me.
Check our SMART goals against our Situation analysis. Which one is weakest?
Here are three post ideas — enter them in our content calendar for 2, 4 and 6 December.

3 · Rules for working with the coach

  • You write the plan. Use Claude for questions, feedback and checks — not to fill in whole sections.
  • Label AI support in Situation › Our Approach and link your chat there ("Links to your AI reports").
  • Check every fact Claude names. Claude can be wrong, especially about numbers and organisations.
  • Your group code opens your plan. Share it only within your team.

If something does not work

  • "No plan found" — check the code: it is the code you type into the tool (upper/lower case does not matter, spaces count as dashes).
  • Claude does not use the connector — switch it on in the chat, or write "use the SOSTAC Coach connector".
  • Claude's text is not in the tool yet — switch back to the tool tab; it updates within a minute. If you were typing in the same field, the tool asks which version to keep.
  • The first message is empty — the button also copies it: paste it into Claude.
